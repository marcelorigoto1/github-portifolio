Hello There!

I am Marcelo Alves Rigoto, a Geographer from Universidade Federal Fluminense and MSc in Geoinformatics, awarded as a joint degree by Palacký University Olomouc (UPOL) and Paris Lodron University of Salzburg (PLUS) through the Copernicus Master in Digital Earth.

My first year in Salzburg built a broad foundation across GIS, remote sensing, and geospatial technologies; in Olomouc I specialized in GeoVisualization, focusing on cartographic design, visual analytics, and spatial storytelling. My master thesis combined NASA FIRMS satellite data with Unity and Cesium to reconstruct wildfire spread as interactive 3D visualizations.

I have over five years of hands-on GIS experience across land administration, municipal, and remote-sensing projects in Brazil — including several years as a GIS Technician at Imagem Geosistemas, developing automated workflows, dashboards, and web maps with Python and ArcGIS Online — plus topography and land-survey fieldwork with drones, TLS, and photogrammetry in Portugal, and 3D geovisualization work with Cesium and Unity3D.
