Topography & Survey Internship

Geotrilho — Barreiro, Portugal · Jul – Aug 2025

Overview

The project combined drone-based and ground-based data acquisition with advanced 3D modeling techniques. Fieldwork was supported by topographic surveys and processed using photogrammetry and specialized software to support infrastructure planning.

Main Activities

  • Data collection with drones (UAV) and terrestrial laser scanners (TLS).
  • Review of technical documentation and workflows.
  • Photogrammetry processing with Agisoft Metashape.
  • 3D modeling and analysis with 3DReshaper.
  • Data integration, processing, and cleaning.

Outcomes

Generated preliminary 3D models, structured workflows, and validated datasets, ready for the production of orthomosaics, digital elevation models, and textured 3D meshes.

Tools

  • Drone (UAV) survey
  • Terrestrial laser scanning
  • Agisoft Metashape
  • 3DReshaper
  • Photogrammetry
